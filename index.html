Why the status titles are strict

We mark claims as OPEN, FORMAL, CLOSED, CLOSED-NEGATIVE, CLOSED-POSITIVE, NUMERICS, or STRUCTURAL ANALOGY so the system cannot behave like a yes-man. The title is a constraint, not decoration.

Each label tells the reader what survived, what failed, what remains owed, and what kind of evidence is being carried. A closed route is preserved because failure is data. An open route is protected because ambition still owes proof. A typed pass is useful only inside its declared scope.

KNS as Light is formal in this exact sense: Light names the visible incidence layer — needles, overlap, glare, and bloom. It does not own Action(C), placement, zero-location, or theorem lift. That proof debt is why the page says OWED.
