Rigor Gates — What Is Closed

The corrected branch map separates three different corridors instead of collapsing them into one.

Theorem B, corrected VERIFIED

The resolved inner product removes the thermal weight and leaves the bare symmetric kernel. Its Hilbert–Schmidt norm converges exactly when σ>1/2. This yields a legitimate compact self-adjoint operator with real discrete spectrum tending to zero.

Corridor I — parity CLOSED

Ξ(z) is even. But Tr(Kσ3) is a strictly positive closed-walk sum. The naive determinant and det₂ expansion carry a cubic term forbidden by Ξ.

Corridor II — counting CLOSED

The n⁴ free spectrum gives N(Λ)≈Λ1/4. Zeta zeros require Riemann–von Mangoldt growth, N(Λ)≈√Λ logΛ after the z²+1/4 substitution. Bounded or relatively compact coupling cannot change the Weyl class.

Trace-class determinant GENUS

If A∈S₁, det(I−zA)=∏(1−zλn) is a genus-0 product. Ξ is order 1 and genus 1. Therefore no trace-class determinant equals C·Ξ(z).

Audit Correction — What V6.4 Overstated

The no-go conclusion remains under strong pressure. The proof language is corrected.

Gap 1 · local prime-free entries do not imply prime-free global invariants

The theta function is built from integer-square data, yet through Riemann's theta representation it carries ζ's analytic continuation and functional equation. Therefore the line “there is nowhere for (log2)/√2 to originate” is heuristic unless a global invariant is computed.

Gap 2 · trace-neutrality is first-order

Theorems C and E constrain the first-order trace channel. A determinant is an all-orders object with cumulants Tr(Kp) for p≥2. First-order trace neutrality does not by itself rule out a finite-coupling determinant identity.

Corrected language

Use: prime-support obstruction is a necessary-condition heuristic and trace-level pressure. Do not use: prime-support obstruction alone proves determinant-level impossibility for every Hilbert–Schmidt realization.

Tr(Kσp) > 0 for every p≥2 is the rigorous positivity invariant.

Hilbert–Schmidt Corridor — Reduced to ρ(Kσ)

This is the next analytic probe before any further repo bridge language.

For A∈S₂, det₂(I−zA) is an entire function of genus at most 1. Since Ξ has order exactly 1, a necessary condition for det₂(I−zKσ) = C·Ξ(z) is that the singular-value convergence exponent equals 1.

ρ(Kσ) = inf{ p≥1 : Σn sn(Kσ)p < ∞ }
necessary condition for det₂(I−zKσ) = C·Ξ(z):   ρ(Kσ) = 1

Next probe: compute or prove asymptotics for sn(Kσ). If ρ(Kσ)≠1, the Hilbert–Schmidt determinant corridor closes rigorously by order. If ρ(Kσ)=1, descend to genus, zero-density, cumulant, and functional-equation checks.
