Closed Branch — What the Kernel Proved, and What It Cannot Carry

The square kernel is kept as a compact-geometry test object, not as an arithmetic bridge.

Theorem B, corrected VERIFIED

The resolved inner product removes the thermal weight and leaves the bare symmetric kernel. Its Hilbert–Schmidt norm converges exactly when σ>1/2. This yields a legitimate compact self-adjoint operator with real discrete spectrum tending to zero.

Parity wall CLOSED

Ξ(z) is even. But Tr(Kσ3) is a strictly positive triangle sum. The naive determinant and det₂ expansion carry a cubic term forbidden by Ξ.

Counting wall CLOSED

The n⁴ free spectrum gives N(Λ)≈Λ1/4. Zeta zeros require Riemann–von Mangoldt growth, N(Λ)≈√Λ logΛ after the z²+1/4 substitution. The operator is too sparse by Weyl class.

Prime-support wall CLOSED

The explicit formula requires Λ(n)/√n supported at log n = log(pk). The kernel |m²−n²| sees square-difference geometry, not Euler-product arithmetic.
