## Claim statuses

### FORMAL

A definition, identity, theorem, or obstruction with an explicit proof or direct derivation.

### PROPOSED

A named bridge or conjectural route with enough structure to be tested, falsified, or refined.

### STRUCTURAL ANALOGY

External mathematics that stabilizes vocabulary or geometric intuition but carries no proof-weight for the zeta target.

### OPEN

A load-bearing theorem burden not yet closed.

### FALSIFIED-IN-REALIZATION

A conjectural formulation whose stated natural operationalization has failed a test.
