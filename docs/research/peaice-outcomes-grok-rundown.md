## What Outcomes is

PeAIce Outcomes is the public v1 publication surface for:

> **When AI Will Not Act as a Partner — KakeyaLogic Applied + Benevolence Drift**

It is a **research-partnership protocol**, not a proof artifact. Governing rule:

```text

```
